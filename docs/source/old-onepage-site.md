WHAT WE DO

## Our Services

The MechLab Engineering is a mechanical design studio that turns ideas into manufacturable products using modern CAD and 3D printing.

### Product Development

From concept and prototype to a production-ready design.

### Mechanical Design

Concept design, mechanisms, machine parts, fixtures and jigs.

### 3D CAD Modelling

Parametric part and assembly models ready for manufacturing.

### Sheet Metal Design

Enclosures, brackets and panels with bend and flat-pattern details.

### 2D Drafting & GD&T

Production drawings with dimensions, tolerances and fits.

### Reverse Engineering

Recreate models and drawings from existing physical parts.

### Fabrication Services

Cutting, bending, welding and assembly of custom parts.

### 3D Printing

Custom prototypes and functional parts in PLA, PETG, ABS and more.
