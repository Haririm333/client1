# Content guardrails

## Source of truth

Facts available: company name, tagline (DESIGN • ENGINEER • DELIVER), email themechlabengineering@gmail.com, phone +91 86102 41235, WhatsApp wa.me/918610241235, the 8 services and their descriptions, the 5-step process, the 5 "why us" points, 3D printing materials (PLA, PETG, ABS "and more"), the 6 representative components, "SolidWorks-style workflow" (catalog p3), © 2026.

## Allowed
Rewording and expanding source statements in plain engineering language. Generic, textbook-true explanations of what a service involves (e.g. what a flat pattern or GD&T drawing is) are fine, flagged `<!-- OWNER REVIEW -->` in the data file.

## Banned (until the owner supplies proof)
Years in business, project or client counts, client names or logos, testimonials, ratings, ISO or other certifications, NDA guarantees (source only says "handled confidentially"), response-time numbers ("24h"), prices, tolerances achieved, build volumes, machine or software lists beyond the source, city/address, team names or bios, awards, guarantees.

## Banned words
unleash, elevate, seamless, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, world-class, revolutionize, game-changing, synergy, holistic, next-level, passionate, tailored solutions, one-stop shop, "in today's fast-paced world".

## Voice
Engineer to engineer. Short, concrete sentences. Say what is delivered (a STEP file, a drawing set, a printed prototype). Active voice. One idea per paragraph. Headlines state the benefit or the thing, not a slogan. Use British/Indian English spelling consistent with the source ("modelling", "sheetmetal" as in source brand copy, "optimised").
